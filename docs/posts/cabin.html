Cabin

a cabin of wonder
a cabin of dreams
away from the city
where nature prevails

you can find there some berries
you can find there some help
among the trees and bushes
you can find there yourself

lake water will rinse down your worries
wood fire will warm up your soul

with sunrise get ready
sandwich in hand
your backpack full of berries
walk steady
into this crazy world
